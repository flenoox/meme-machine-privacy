# Privacy Policy — Meme Machine

**Last updated:** 2026-10-26

## Summary

Meme Machine does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing happens locally in your browser.

## Data we process

| Data | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Images | Locally in the browser | Rendered on canvas for meme creation |
| Language preference | `storage.local` (your browser) | To remember RU / EN choice |

**We do not send any data to any server.**

## Permissions

- `contextMenus` — to show "Meme now!" in the right-click menu.
- `storage` — to save your language preference.
- `activeTab` — to capture a screenshot of the current tab (only when you click "Screenshot").
- `scripting` — to inject a helper script that reads the image `src`.
- `<all_urls>` — required for the right-click menu to work on any image on any site. No background scanning.

## Third parties

We do **not** use:
- analytics (Google Analytics, Yandex, etc.);
- advertising networks;
- external AI services;
- user accounts or authentication.

Social sharing opens links to external sites (Telegram, VK, etc.) — we do not control their privacy policies.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data (language preference).
